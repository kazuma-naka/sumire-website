# Sumire claymorphism direction

Applied on 2026-10-05. Keep the violet identity and readable Japanese typography, with inflated keycaps, mint and peach supporting colors, and soft inner highlights plus outer shadows. Give the marketing pages the strongest depth; keep the settings guide and forms compact and readable.

Reference: [Hype4 / SquarePlanet claymorphism generator](https://hype4.academy/tools/claymorphism-generator), which demonstrates the layered shadow technique. The website artwork is original, not copied from reference sites.

## Assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, on transparent backgrounds. WebP exports preserve alpha; no runtime image service is required. The hero has a smaller responsive export. The four exports total approximately 275 KB.

- `assets/clay-keyboard.webp` and `assets/clay-keyboard-small.webp`: hero and features introduction.
- `assets/clay-words.webp`: dictionaries and words.
- `assets/clay-personalize.webp`: personalization.

The illustrations communicate the product's personality. Real, unmodified app screenshots remain in the accessible layout selector and the tablet and conversion examples.

## Generation prompts

Create one cohesive premium 3D matte clay still life, square composition, isolated on genuinely transparent background. Only three large clear key labels: Japanese あ, Japanese す, Latin A; other keys blank or small simple dots. A warm pastel apricot smiling round speech bubble floats behind the upper right, a little mint chat bubble lower left, and a small five-petaled violet flower nestled near the keyboard upper left, referencing the name Sumire. One or two loose chunky keycaps and a tiny soft yellow four-point sparkle complete the composition. Calm but delightful, tactile sculpted clay with soft bevels and slight handmade imperfection, studio-quality ambient occlusion, light from upper left, lavender tinted soft shadows, no harsh shine. Large readable forms, pleasing asymmetry, complete object fully inside frame with generous padding. Main keyboard is the dominant subject. Colors: lavender and deeper Sumire violet, creamy white, pastel mint, pale peach, butter yellow. No webpage, no surrounding UI, no headings or text beyond the three specified key labels, no real phone/device brand, no watermark. The illustration represents joyful Japanese typing, not a screenshot or actual product interface.

## Interaction

The small key playground changes a localized message on activation. It is separate from the screenshot selector. Both use native buttons and live status text. Interactive controls stay hidden until JavaScript initialization. All content and the default kana screenshot remain visible without JavaScript. Motion is limited to presses, a short message pop, screenshot crossfades, and one-time section entrances; reduced-motion preferences remove it.
